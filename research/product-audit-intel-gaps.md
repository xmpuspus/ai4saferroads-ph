## What would take it from demo to credible tool

1. **Crash validation** (High) — join DRIVER/PSA crashes, show flagged corridors over-index on injuries. Without this it's a hypothesis.
2. **Fix the one-way/divided classifier and add real intersection density** (Critical/High) — the two structural model errors that produce silent false negatives and a narrated-but-absent capability.
3. **Reconcile the sim claims and the e2e suite** (Critical) — stop calling a 2D canvas a "three.js 3D satellite sim" and get `make e2e` green before any "all pass" claim.
4. **Stop the map from overstating risk** (High) — distinguish imputed from real-posted on the glow/traffic, not just in the count.
5. **Ship the before/after view + data download + deep-links** (Medium) — the policy-actionable surfaces that make it a tool, not a poster.

## SCORES

**Intelligence: 52 / 100.**
The sourced spine is genuinely strong — Safe System thresholds, the Nilsson 4th-power model, the
Tefft curve, the fatal-risk-reduction math, and the gap-floor invariant (E can't invent risk) are
all correct, cited, and reproduce on recomputation. The grade-separation guard (Skyway fix) is good
defensive design. But the classifier has two structural defects that produce silent false negatives
(`oneway=yes` misread as divided → V_safe inflated to 70; intersection density claimed but never
computed), the score is a coarse 6-value ladder masquerading as 0–100, imputed limits (81–98% of
data) are scored and mapped on equal footing with measured ones, and a class-error false positive
(EDSA Busway) sits in the headline. Honest where it counts in the *numbers*, overstated in the *map*.

**Feature Gaps: 38 / 100.**
The differentiator is real and conservatively framed, and replicability across 3 cities is proven.
But against iRAP / USLIMITS2 / GRSF it is missing every table-stakes that separates a tool from a
visualization: no crash-data validation, no operating-speed layer (the axis those tools are built
on), no before/after recommended-limit view (its own marketed feature), no search / deep-links /
data export, no temporal exposure, no P2W layer (the stated APAC hook), and a single ~6×6 km AOI
mislabeled as a full metro. Strong concept, thin product surface.
